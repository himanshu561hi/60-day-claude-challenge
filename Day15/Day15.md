# Day 15: Advanced Prompt Engineering - Personal Life Analysis Consultant

## Project Overview

For Day 15 of the ABTalks 60-Day Coding Challenge, the objective was to explore advanced prompt engineering techniques, specifically focusing on **structured report generation** using Large Language Models (LLMs).

Using a highly structured system prompt, I configured an LLM to act as an expert Vedic Astrologer persona to generate a comprehensive life pattern and career analysis report based on specific input parameters.

---

## Input Parameters Provided

- **Full Name:** Himanshu Gupta
- **Gender:** Male
- **Date of Birth:** January 25, 2005
- **Exact Birth Time:** *[Insert your birth time here, e.g., 04:30 PM]*
- **Birth Time Accuracy:** *[Exact/Approximate/Unknown]*
- **Place of Birth:** Place
- **Current City:** City
- **Relationship Status:** Married/Unmarried
- **Profession:** B.Tech Student / Full-Stack Developer
- **Top 3 Current Concerns:**
  1. When can I expect to secure a job in the tech industry?
  2. Should I pursue a Software Engineer role at an MNC, or would it be better to build my own tech product or startup?
  3. What does my chart indicate regarding my longevity and critical life phases?

---

## Core Prompts & Methodology

The prompt utilizes a complex persona focusing on Parashara, Jaimini, Nakshatra, Vimshottari Dasha, and Transit Analysis. It enforces strict output constraints, requiring:

- A structured Birth Chart Summary.
- In-depth Life Pattern and Career/Wealth Analysis.
- A clean, Markdown-formatted **5-Year Forecast Table** spanning Career, Money, Relationships, and Health.
- Actionable, astrologically justified remedies.

This exercise perfectly demonstrates how setting clear boundaries, professional personas, and strict formatting rules can turn an ambiguous LLM response into a production-ready, highly tailored user report.
