# Day 22: Validating Student Project Marketplace Like a VC

## Overview

Today's challenge focused on moving away from assumption-based building and moving toward data-driven validation. By acting as a Startup Advisor and VC Analyst, I evaluated the core concepts behind the **Student Project Marketplace** against real market metrics, sizing frameworks, and potential risks before writing a single line of code.

## Core Metrics & Insights

The validation framework returned a clear picture of the opportunity and the execution hurdles ahead:

* **Problem Severity:** `9 / 10` — Bridging the gap for Tier-2 and Tier-3 students lacking real-world experience is a critical pain point.
* **Founder-Market Fit Score:** `6.75 / 10` — Strong engineering background, but requires deeper operational channels with startups/SMEs.
* **Market Sizing (SAM):** `$1.45 Billion` — Significant room for capturing localized value within the experiential learning sector.
* **Overall Verdict:** **Conditional Go** — High potential, but contingent on immediate user and corporate validation.

---

## The Core Hypotheses

### 💡 The Problem

1. **The Experience Gap:** Students struggle to land jobs due to a lack of verified, real-world project history.
2. **Resource Constraints:** Startups and SMEs need affordable talent but cannot compete with enterprise packages.
3. **The Tier Divide:** Capable students in Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities are structurally isolated from premium ecosystem opportunities.

### 🎯 The Vision

Build a collaborative decentralized ecosystem where students team up to ship actual products for real organizations, bypassing typical systemic gatekeepers to construct robust, unforgeable proof-of-work portfolios.
