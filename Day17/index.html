The Paradox Explained

E85 is 28.2% cheaper at the pump. However, ethanol has ~33% less energy than petrol per litre, so the XUV700 consumes ~16% more fuel per km. Net result: 15.2% running cost advantage — far more impactful than on a small bike. At 1200 km/month this translates to ₹1,332 saved monthly.

  • Flex-fuel kit fitted
❌ E85 Risks
  • Voids Mahindra warranty
  • Cold-start rich mixture
  • Rare pumps outside MH/KA
