Stage 1: Lead Capture & Qualification

Est. Time: 15 mins (Automated)

Objectives

  • Capture prospective client data automatically.
  • Filter out low-budget or out-of-scope inquiries.
  • Set a highly professional first impression.

Tasks

Expected Outputs

A filtered list of high-intent, budget-approved leads booked on your calendar without manual emailing.

AI Tools & Recommendations

Typeform + Zapier + ChatGPT Primary

Why: Typeform offers a conversational UI. Zapier connects it to your CRM. ChatGPT can evaluate the responses to classify the lead's quality.

Alternatives: Tally Forms, Make.com, Claude 3 API

AI Prompt Example

You are an expert sales assistant. Review the following client inquiry form submission: [Insert Form Data] Task: 1. Determine if the budget aligns with our minimum of $5,000. 2. Determine if the requested timeline (X weeks) is feasible. 3. Draft a professional, enthusiastic email response. If qualified, include my booking link [Link]. If unqualified, decline politely and recommend they check out my DIY resources at [Link].
Best Practices & Common Mistakes
Best Practices:
  • Always ask for budget ranges upfront.
  • Keep the initial form under 7 questions to reduce friction.
  • Use conditional logic to hide/show questions based on previous answers.
Common Mistakes:
  • Asking for too much detail before a discovery call.
  • Failing to send an immediate auto-responder acknowledging receipt.
  • Not syncing the form directly to a CRM, causing data silos.

Stage 2: Discovery & Proposal Generation

Est. Time: 45 mins

Objectives

  • Understand client pain points deeply during the call.
  • Instantly translate call transcripts into a compelling proposal.
  • Deliver a tailored strategy that justifies premium pricing.

Tasks

AI Tools & Recommendations

Otter.ai / Fireflies + Claude 3.5 Sonnet Primary

Why: Fireflies records and transcribes the call. Claude 3.5 Sonnet is currently the best at natural language generation for business writing, turning raw transcripts into structured proposals perfectly.

Alternatives: Fathom, Proposify, PandaDoc

AI Prompt Example

Here is the transcript from a discovery call with a new client: [Insert Transcript] Act as a senior agency copywriter. Extract the client's main pain points, desired outcomes, and required deliverables. Draft a 3-page proposal layout including: 1. Executive Summary 2. Proposed Solution (3 phases) 3. Timeline & Investment Use persuasive, confident, and professional language.
Efficiency Tip

Create a master Template in PandaDoc or Notion. Use AI only to generate the custom text blocks (Executive summary, specific deliverables). Copy-paste the AI output into your beautiful template rather than having AI try to format the whole document.
