76 / 100

Student Project Marketplace is a structurally sound business hypothesis with genuinely favorable market timing, a credible monetization model, and a founder distribution advantage that funded competitors cannot easily replicate. The project-first + Tier-2/3 geography + workflow automation combination is genuinely uncontested, and Internshala's upstream move has opened a measurable, time-limited window.

However, this startup does not yet qualify as a sustainable business. Not a single organization has paid. Not a single student has completed a platform-mediated project. Every revenue number, every GTM assumption, and every claim about org willingness-to-pay is a hypothesis dressed as a plan. The two-sided cold-start problem is unsolved and the validation sprint has not begun.

The path to sustainability is precise: Execute the 30-day sprint (20+ interviews, 5+ org calls, 3 project briefs, 1 real invoice ask). If org willingness-to-pay at ₹999/month is confirmed by 3 independent founders → build the 3-feature MVP. If not → reprice before investing a single hour of engineering time. The idea is not the risk. Execution discipline is the only risk that matters now.
