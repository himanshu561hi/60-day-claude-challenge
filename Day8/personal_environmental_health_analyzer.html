  • 🌍Varanasi ranks 15th most polluted city globally today, despite AQI figures that are common across northern India — highlighting how severe the Indo-Gangetic Plain pollution crisis has become.
  • 📈Biggest anomaly: Kolkata AQI at 138 is unusually high for a coastal city in summer. Pre-monsoon humidity and industrial output from the Hooghly belt are likely drivers.
Recommended Actions for Varanasi Residents
Daily Actions
Wear N95/KN95 mask outdoors, especially 7–10 AM when AQI peaks (max recorded 175 at 7:31 AM)
Keep windows closed during peak pollution hours; use exhaust fans only at night
Use a HEPA air purifier indoors — prioritise the bedroom for 8+ hrs of cleaner sleep air
Outdoor Activity Guidance
Exercise only between 6–8 PM when AQI is lowest (AQI ~69 at 6 PM vs 175 at morning)
Avoid areas near Ghats, roads, and construction sites — PM levels are 2–3× higher there
Hair & Skin Care
Install a shower filter (activated carbon + KDF) to reduce hardness, chlorine, and heavy metals before water contacts skin and hair
Use sulfate-free, pH-balanced shampoo (5.5 pH) — hard water raises hair shaft pH causing breakage
Apply a ceramide or hyaluronic moisturiser immediately after showering to seal moisture before PM2.5 deposits can trigger oxidative stress on skin
Weekly deep-conditioning hair mask to counter mineral buildup and dryness from high-TDS water
Water Improvement
Use a RO+UV+TDS controller filter for drinking water — Ganga-sourced supply exceeds WHO TDS and coliform limits
Never consume water without treatment — research confirms biological contamination at Varanasi ghats year-round
