🚀 Day 14: Career Intelligence Showcase | 60-Day Coding Challenge

Job hunting is a massive investment of time and energy. But how do you know if a job description is a hidden gem or a recipe for immediate burnout?

For **Day 14**, I built an **AI Job Red Flag Detector**! 🛠️🤖

This tool analyzes job listings, company text, and postings to uncover subtle warnings before you hit "Apply." It flags phrases like "fast-paced environment" or "wear many hats" when they actually mean poor structure, while simultaneously highlighting genuine "green flags" like clear mentorship and healthy boundaries.

**Key Takeaways from Day 14:**

* Using AI to evaluate career data objectively.
* Designing prompt logic to act as an expert recruiter and career coach.
* Building tools that save time and empower engineering job seekers.
