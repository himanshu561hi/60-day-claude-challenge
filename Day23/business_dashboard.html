Sustainability Verdict
🟡
VALIDATE
74 / 100

Student Project Marketplace has the structural ingredients of a sustainable business — a severe, persistent, large-scale problem (1.5M+ affected annually), a clear two-sided market with motivated buyers and sellers, a monetization model with believable unit economics, and a strategic timing window created by Internshala's upstream move.

However, sustainability cannot be confirmed without completing the validation sprint. No org has paid. No student has completed a project. No structured discovery interviews have been conducted. The two-sided cold-start problem and org willingness-to-pay remain the highest-impact unknowns.

Final Call: Execute the 30-day validation sprint with extreme discipline. If org willingness-to-pay is confirmed → pre-seed investable. If not → pricing model requires fundamental rethink before further investment of time or capital.
